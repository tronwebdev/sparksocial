# AG Accessories and Jewelries — Brand Knowledge Base

Everything the brand has confirmed, written down so it can be cited — and,
just as importantly, what it has **not** confirmed.

---

## What we are

A fashion accessories and jewelry brand. We sell jewelry and complementary
fashion accessories chosen to suit an outfit, an occasion and a person.

**Slogan** Elegance in Every Detail.
**Identity** Black and metallic gold — sophistication, confidence, timeless style.
**Positioning** Premium-looking, accessible elegance.

**We are a retailer.** We select and sell; we do not manufacture, design or
assemble what we sell.

These are the brand's **proposed** categories, not confirmed inventory. Verify a
category is actually stocked before a post advertises it.

- **Jewelry** — necklaces and pendants, earrings, rings, bracelets and bangles,
  jewelry sets.
- **Fashion accessories** — watches, sunglasses, handbags, hair accessories.
- **Gifting** — selections and coordinated accessories for birthdays,
  anniversaries and celebrations.

No price range has been confirmed for any category.

## Who we serve

Fashion-conscious women and men. In practice:

- people buying everyday jewelry and accessories
- people preparing for a party, wedding or celebration
- people buying a gift for a partner, friend, family member or colleague
- people coordinating accessories with an outfit for work or an event

Do not assume every product suits every customer. Recommend on the basis of
need, occasion, preference and budget.

## How we talk

Elegant but plain. Warm, confident, inviting, professional.

We make a customer feel stylish and valued. We do not push. We are concise on a
simple question and genuinely helpful on a comparison.

We never make someone feel that an expensive item is necessary in order to look
attractive.

## Things we do not say

**Materials and authenticity — the big one.** Never claim a piece is solid gold,
sterling silver, set with genuine gemstones, or hypoallergenic, unless that is
verified for that specific item. Where the material is unknown, say so.

- Never "solid gold", "real gold", "sterling silver", "genuine gemstones",
  "precious stones", "hypoallergenic", "nickel-free".
- Never "luxury house", "designer brand", "authorized retailer", "certified" —
  the brand is luxury-*inspired*, not a certified luxury house or an authorised
  designer retailer.
- Never "handmade", "handcrafted", "our workshop", "designed in-house",
  "artisan", "we make". **We are a retailer and do not manufacture anything.**
- Never "lowest price", "cheapest", "best price guaranteed".
- Never "guaranteed" about durability, tarnish, longevity or appearance.
- Never "investment piece" or "holds its value".
- Never state a price, discount, promotion, stock level, delivery time, payment
  method or return term — none are confirmed.
- Never invent an address, phone number, WhatsApp number, email or opening hours.
- Never claim a customer review, testimonial, certification or business
  registration.
- No pressure selling: "last chance", "selling fast", "don't miss out",
  "limited time", "hurry".
- Nothing about the wearer's body — no "flattering", "slimming", "elongating".

- The pieces themselves, photographed.

do not make what we sell, and a post needing a making-of shot is one this
business cannot truthfully produce.
