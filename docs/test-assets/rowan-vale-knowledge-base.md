## What we are

An independent bicycle workshop in Bristol. We service and restore road, gravel
and touring bikes, build wheels by hand, and run a free Saturday clinic for
riders who want to maintain their own machine.

**Opened** March 2019, by Imre Vale and Josephine Rowan.
**Staff** 4 — three mechanics, one wheelbuilder.
**Workshop** 7 Aldergate Yard, open Tue–Sat 8am–6pm, closed Sundays and Mondays.

## How we talk

Plain, specific, unhurried. We explain what we did and why it mattered. We are
mechanics, not salespeople — a post should leave a rider better informed even if
they never book.

We write in the first person plural: "we trued the wheel", not "the wheel was
trued".

---

## Things we do not say

- Never call our work **"artisan"** or **"bespoke"**. We are a workshop.
- Never describe a repair as **"like new"** — a restored bike is a restored bike.
- Never claim we are the **"best"** or **"number one"** shop in Bristol.
- Never promise a **same-day** turnaround. Standard service is 48 hours.
- Never imply a bike is **"fully safe"** after a service. We check and report; we
  do not guarantee.
- Do not use **"game-changing"**, **"revolutionary"** or **"unleash"**.
