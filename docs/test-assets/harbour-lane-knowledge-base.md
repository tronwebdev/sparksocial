## What we are

A neighbourhood bakery on Harbour Lane. We bake sourdough and pastries fresh
each morning and supply four local cafés.

**Opened** June 2022, by Nell Okafor.
**Staff** 5 — two bakers, two front of house, one driver.
**Shop** 12 Harbour Lane, open Tue–Sun 7am–2pm, closed Mondays.

## How we bake

The starter is four years old and came from Nell's home kitchen. It is fed twice
daily with flour from Trenoweth Mill, thirty miles away.

Dough is mixed at 4am, bulk-proved through the morning, shaped in the afternoon
and cold-retarded overnight — **18 hours** from mix to oven. That is why the
crust cracks the way it does, and why we cannot make more at short notice.

Pastries are laminated over two days. Saturday's cinnamon buns are the only item
we make once a week, because they need the oven free at 5am.

## Who buys from us

**Morning regulars.** The largest group. They collect between 7 and 9am, usually
the same order each time, and they care that it is ready when they said.

**Weekend families.** Bigger baskets, later, more pastries. They will queue.

**The four cafés.** They care about delivery time and consistency, not tasting
notes. A late delivery costs them a morning.

## How we talk

Plain and warm. We name the time, the temperature and the number of hours,
because those are the facts that separate us from a supermarket loaf.

**We say:** "Mixed at four, in your hands by eight." · "Eighteen hours, most of
it spent doing nothing." · "Forty slots, then we are out."

**We do not say:** "artisan", "hand-crafted", "passion for baking", "elevate
your morning". Every one of them could describe anybody.

We do not use exclamation marks in captions.

## Things we must never claim

- **No health claims.** Not "gut-friendly", not "easier to digest", not
  low-GI. Sourdough is bread we sell, not a supplement.
- **Not "organic".** Trenoweth flour is not certified and neither are we.
- **No allergen safety claims.** We bake wheat, rye, butter and nuts in one small
  room. We cannot promise anything is free of any of them.
- **No comparison claims** about named bakeries.
- **No "award-winning"** — we have won nothing.

## Frequently asked

**Do you sell out?** Most Saturdays by 11am. Forty collection slots are held back
for orders placed the night before.

**Can I freeze it?** Yes, on the day. Slice first, freeze in a bag, toast from
frozen.

**Is there gluten-free?** No, and we will not pretend otherwise — see allergens
above.

**Do you deliver?** Wholesale only, before 6:30am. No home delivery.

**Why closed Mondays?** The starter gets a quiet day and so does everyone else.

## The year, roughly

**January–February** quiet; wholesale carries it.
**March** anniversary of opening.
**June–August** pastries overtake bread; tourists.
**October** the best baking weather of the year — cool kitchen, slow prove.
**December** hampers and pre-orders; a third of annual revenue in three weeks.
