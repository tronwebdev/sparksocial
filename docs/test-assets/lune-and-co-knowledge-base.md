## What we are

A jewellery and accessories **shop**. We buy in and sell earrings, necklaces,
bracelets, rings and gift sets. We do not make anything, we do not design
anything, and we do not alter anything.

**Opened** February 2023, by Priya Lune.
**Staff** 4 — all shopfloor and packing. Nobody here is a jeweller.
**Shop** Unit 12, Northgate Arcade, Leeds. Open Mon–Sat 9.30am–6pm, Sun 11am–4pm.
**Online** Same stock, same prices, next-day dispatch before 2pm.

---

## What we actually do

We choose what to stock, we photograph it, we sell it, we pack it nicely and we
handle the returns. That is the whole job and we are good at it.

We buy from twelve suppliers. We do not name them publicly — it is the one thing
that genuinely is commercially sensitive here.

## How we talk

Friendly and useful. We help people choose a gift and we are good at that —
most of our customers are buying for someone else and are not confident about
jewellery.

We talk about what a piece looks like, who it suits, what it comes with and when
it will arrive. Those are the things we actually know.

## Things we do not say

**The big one: we are not makers.** Never "handmade", "handcrafted", "made by
us", "our workshop", "designed in-house", "our artisans", "crafted". We buy
finished stock from suppliers and sell it. Any post implying otherwise is false
and it is the single easiest mistake to make about this business.

Also never:

- Never claim a metal, carat, plating thickness or stone type unless the
  supplier states it on the product page. Usually they do not. "Gold-tone" is
  the honest phrase when we do not know.
- Never "hypoallergenic", "nickel-free" or "won't tarnish" — we have not tested
  anything and we cannot stand behind it.
- Never "solid gold", "real silver" or "fine jewellery". This is fashion
  jewellery at fashion jewellery prices.
- Never "ethically sourced" or "sustainable". We do not audit twelve suppliers
  and saying so would be a guess dressed as a fact.
- Never "limited edition" unless a line is genuinely discontinued and we have
  said so internally first.
- Never "investment" or "heirloom".
- Never anything about the wearer's body — no "flattering", "slimming",
  "elongating".
- No false urgency: "last chance", "almost gone", "selling fast", "don't miss
  out". We restock most lines.
- Never name a supplier.
