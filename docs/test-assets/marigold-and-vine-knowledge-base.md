## What we are

A women's jewellery and accessories studio in Manchester. We design demi-fine
jewellery, print and hand-roll silk scarves, and make hair pieces. Everything is
designed here; the jewellery is cast by a workshop in Birmingham and finished,
set and quality-checked on our own bench.

**Opened** May 2020, by Ottoline Vine, who still designs every piece.
**Staff** 5 — two on the bench, one on silk, two on the shopfront and orders.
**Studio** 9 Marigold Walk, Ancoats. Open Thu–Sun 11am–6pm; the bench is behind glass.

## How we talk

Warm and direct. We assume the reader is buying for herself, not being bought
for — most of our orders are self-purchase, and writing as though every piece is
a gift from someone else gets that wrong.

We are specific about materials because the category is full of vagueness. "Gold
vermeil, 2.5 microns over recycled sterling" is the sentence; "luxurious gold
finish" is not.

We talk about wear honestly. Plating wears. Silk creases. Saying so and offering
the fix is more persuasive than pretending otherwise.

## Things we do not say

- Never "solid gold" or "fine jewellery" — we are demi-fine and vermeil, and the
  distinction is legally meaningful.
- Never "hypoallergenic" as a blanket claim. Say "nickel-free" where that is
  documented.
- Never "tarnish-proof" or "waterproof". Say how long the plating lasts and that
  redipping is £15.
- Never "handmade in Manchester" about the jewellery — it is cast in Birmingham
  and finished here. "Designed and finished in Manchester" is the true sentence.
- Never "investment piece" or "will hold its value". It will not; that is not
  what it is for.
- Never "flattering", "slimming", or anything about the wearer's body.
- Never "treat yourself" framing, or urgency: "last chance", "almost gone",
  "don't miss out".
- Never imply a piece is limited when it is a repeating line. Say the batch size
  if it is genuinely a batch.
