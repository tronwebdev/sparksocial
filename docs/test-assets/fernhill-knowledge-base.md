## What we are

Fernhill Roastery is a small-batch coffee roaster with one café on Fernhill Road
and a subscription that ships nationwide.

We roast single-origin coffee twice a week, sell it over the counter, and post it
to subscribers the morning after it comes off the roaster.

**Founded** March 2021, by Ada Cheng and Marcus Bell.
**Staff** 7 — two roasters, four baristas, one operations.
**Roastery and café** 14 Fernhill Road, open Tue–Sun 7am–4pm, closed Mondays.

## Where the coffee comes from

We buy direct from four farms and visit each at least once every two years.

**Finca La Esperanza — Huila, Colombia.** Caturra and Pink Bourbon, 1,750m.
Washed. Bought direct since 2022. Notes: red apple, panela, orange zest.

**Kirinyaga Estate — Kenya.** SL28 and Ruiru 11, 1,600m. Washed. Since 2023.
Notes: blackcurrant, tomato leaf, brown sugar.

**Wush Wush Gardens — Jimma, Ethiopia.** Heirloom varietals, 1,900m. Natural
process. Since 2021, our first direct relationship. Notes: blueberry, jasmine,
apricot.

**Fazenda Boa Vista — Cerrado, Brazil.** Yellow Catuaí, 1,100m. Pulped natural.
Since 2024. Notes: hazelnut, milk chocolate, malt. This is the espresso base.

We pay above the Fairtrade minimum on all four. We are **not** Fairtrade
certified — we buy direct instead, and we should say that plainly rather than
implying a certification we do not hold.

## How we roast

Two roast days: **Tuesday and Friday**, on a 15kg Probat drum roaster, batches
capped at 12kg so the profile stays even.

Subscription orders are packed the same afternoon and collected by the courier at
5pm. A bag posted Tuesday is usually drunk by Thursday.

Bags are one-way valve, recyclable kraft, printed with the origin, the varietal,
the process and **the roast date** — not a best-before. We think the roast date is
the honest number.

## Who buys from us

**Home brewers who own a grinder.** The largest group. They care about origin,
roast date and grind advice, and they will read a long caption about processing
if it teaches them something.

**Office managers.** Buying for a team of 8–40. They care about reliability,
invoicing and the grinder loan, not tasting notes.

**Café walk-ins.** Local, mostly repeat, the source of most of our wholesale
leads — someone drinks it, then asks who roasts it.

## How we talk

Warm and specific. We name the farm, the varietal and the roast date, because
those are the facts that separate us from a supermarket bag.

**We say:** "Roasted Tuesday, with you Thursday." · "Pink Bourbon from Huila,
1,750m." · "Ground for your brewer, or whole bean."

**We do not say:** "artisanal", "hand-crafted", "passionate about coffee",
"third wave", "elevate your morning ritual". Every one of them could describe
anybody, which makes them worth nothing.

We do not use exclamation marks in captions. We do not write in all lower case.

## Things we must never claim

- **No health claims.** Not antioxidants, not metabolism, not "good for you".
  Coffee is a drink we sell, not a supplement.
- **No unsourced origin stories.** If we cannot name the farm and the year, we do
  not tell the story.
- **Not "organic" or "Fairtrade"** — we hold neither certification.
- **No comparison claims** about named competitors.
- **No "world's best" or "award-winning"** unless we name the award and the year.
  We have one: Regional Roaster of the Year, 2025, North division.

## Frequently asked

**How long does coffee stay good?** Best between 5 and 21 days off roast for
filter; espresso we would leave 10 days before pulling it. The roast date is on
every bag.

**Do you grind?** Yes, to order, free. Tell us your brewer. Whole bean keeps
longer, so we default to it.

**Can I pause a subscription?** Any time, from the account page, no notice.
Holiday pauses are the most common reason and we would rather you paused than
cancelled.

**Do you ship outside the UK?** Not yet. Customs and freshness together make it
a worse product than we want to sell.

**Do you do wholesale?** Yes — 18 accounts currently. Minimum 4kg a month,
includes setup and staff training.

**Is there decaf?** One, rotating, Swiss Water process. Currently the Colombian.
