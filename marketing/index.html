The capture loop

When it needs footage, it asks — on WhatsApp

Most small businesses have no media team and no time. So instead of waiting for a shoot, SparkSocial sends a short shot list to your phone. You film it in a few minutes, send it back, and it does the editing.

  • A brief written for a phone camera, not a film crew
  • Send clips straight back in the same chat
  • Cut, captioned and branded for you
  • If a clip will not work, it says so and asks again

Comments and DMs

Publishing is half of it. Replying is the other half.

SparkSocial reads what comes back, sorts it, and drafts replies in your voice. The ones worth money get flagged rather than buried.

  • Sorted by what it is — a question, a complaint, a buying signal
  • Replies drafted for you to approve, never sent blind
  • Anything sensitive escalates to a person instead of being answered
  • A genuine enquiry becomes a lead you can follow up

You choose how far it goes — from watching only, up to drafting every reply.

Analytics that change something

What worked feeds back into what gets made

Most tools show you a chart and stop. SparkSocial takes the result and adjusts the mix it plans next month — and tells you what it changed.

    Publish

    Posts go out with what they were about and why they were chosen.

  2. 02

    Measure

    Reach, engagement and click-throughs come back per post and per campaign.

  3. 03

    Reweight

    Formats that earned attention get more of next month’s slots.

  4. 04

    Explain

    It says what it shifted and on what evidence — and you can freeze it.

Learning can be switched off entirely if you would rather the mix stayed where you put it.
