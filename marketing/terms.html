2. What SparkSocial provides

SparkSocial is a workspace that:

  • builds a profile of your brand — its voice, offer, audience and rules — from what you tell it and from a website address you supply;
  • monitors supported public sources for conversations and scores them for relevance to that brand;
  • drafts content shaped for the platform you are posting to;
  • stores and organises your media, tracking usage rights and reuse;
  • plans a calendar and groups content into campaigns;
  • publishes approved content to accounts you have connected;
  • retrieves comments and messages, classifies them and drafts replies;
  • reports on results and can adjust the planned mix accordingly.

We add, change and occasionally remove features. Where a change materially reduces a feature you rely on, we will give you reasonable notice.
